Privacy Policy

rclone — Google Drive sync application

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who we are

This is the privacy policy for the rclone OAuth application, used only to let the account owner synchronise their own files with their own Google Drive. Contact: minushavishmika@gmail.com.

What we access

When you approve access on Google's consent screen, rclone receives permission to see, edit, create and delete files in your Google Drive. This access exists only so that files can be copied between your computer and your Drive.

What we store

An authorisation token is stored locally on your own computer (inside the rclone configuration file) so that synchronisation can continue without asking for your password again. We do not run a server that keeps your Google data or your token.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share, or publish your files or your Drive contents.
  • We do not receive your Google password — sign-in happens on Google's own pages.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this site.
  • We do not transfer your data to any third party.

Your choices

You can remove this application's access at any time from your Google Account: Google Account → Third-party apps with account access. You may also delete the local configuration file to remove the stored token.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
