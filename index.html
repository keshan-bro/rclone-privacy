rclone

OAuth application — Google Drive sync

This site is the home page for the rclone OAuth client used to authorise rclone command-line tool to access a single user's own Google Drive.

  • The application requests access to your Google Drive so that rclone can synchronise your own files between your computer and your Drive.
  • It is used by the account owner only. No third-party services receive your Google data.
  • Authorisation is granted through Google's own consent screen; this application never sees or stores your Google password.
